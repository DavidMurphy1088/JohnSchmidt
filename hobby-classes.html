Hobbyist Violin Classes

John Schmidt

Weekly Sessions · Wellington

Outside of directing the Wellington School of Violin Making, John also welcomes hobbyists and enthusiasts who simply want to experience the craft of building a violin by hand — without committing to a professional, multi-year programme.

Each week, John guides a small group through the same traditional techniques used by his full-time students: wood selection, carving, purfling, arching, and varnishing — taught at a relaxed, hobbyist pace.

Whether you're a retired engineer, a weekend woodworker, or simply violin-curious, John's sessions are a welcoming way to learn a centuries-old craft alongside other enthusiasts.
